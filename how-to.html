Writing Your First Song ▼
Steps
  1. Enter Topic / Concept — be specific. "Losing my dog after 12 years" works better than "sadness."
  2. Select Genre.
  3. Select Moods — you can pick multiple.
  4. Choose Song Structure.
  5. Set Tempo.
  6. Choose Rhyme Scheme.
  7. Set Point of View.
  8. Optionally add Words to Include, Style Notes, Write in the Style of an artist name, and Language.
  9. Click Write This Song.
Tips
  • More specific topic = better lyrics.
  • Combining moods gives more nuanced results.
  • The Style of field is powerful — try different artists for the same song.

Refining Your Song ▼
Regenerate
Click Regenerate in the action bar for a completely new version using the same settings.
Edit Directly
Click anywhere in the lyrics paper area to edit text. Changes save automatically.
Version History
Click Versions in the action bar to see all previous versions of your song and restore any of them.
Suggested Titles
Three title suggestions appear above the lyrics after generating. Click any to copy to clipboard.
Saving

Songs save automatically to Song History for Pro and Unlimited users. Access from the history icon in the sidebar.

Chord Progressions ▼
Viewing Chords
The Chord Progression section below the lyrics shows the key, strumming pattern, and chords for each section of your song.
Show Chords
Click Show Chords to display chord names above each lyric line in lead sheet format. The chord name appears in gold above the line where the chord change happens.
Transposing
Use the ▼ ▲ arrows next to the key name to shift all chords up or down one semitone. The Guitar Chord Guide updates in real time.
Guitar Chord Guide
SVG fretboard diagrams appear below the lyrics for every unique chord in the song when Show Chords is enabled.
Exporting to Suno ▼
What is Suno?

Suno (suno.com) is an AI music generator. WriteMyLyrics writes the lyrics and chord progressions — Suno generates the full musical track.

Steps
  1. Generate your lyrics in WriteMyLyrics.
  2. Click the Suno button in the action bar above the lyrics.
  3. Copy the formatted lyrics and style prompt that appears.
  4. Go to suno.com and paste into a new song creation.
  5. Suno generates the full song with music.
Saving Your Suno URL

After Suno generates your song, copy the audio URL and paste it into the Suno Audio URL field in the Song Notes panel. This saves it to your song permanently and enables the audio player in the Gallery.

Karaoke Mode ▼
What is Karaoke Mode?

Karaoke Mode highlights your lyrics in real time as music plays, word by word. Requires a direct audio URL (not a Suno share page link).

Getting a Direct URL from Suno

Right-click the audio player in Suno and select Copy audio address — use the direct .mp3 URL, not the song page URL.

Using Karaoke Mode
  1. Open your song in the studio.
  2. Click the Karaoke icon in the sidebar.
  3. Paste the direct audio URL or upload an MP3 file.
  4. Click Auto Sync — Whisper AI automatically matches your lyrics to the audio. Takes 10–30 seconds.
  5. Click Play — lyrics highlight word by word in sync with the music.
  6. Click Save Sync to save the timing permanently to your song.
Note: Karaoke Mode with Auto Sync requires an Unlimited plan.
TikTok Hook Generator ▼
What It Does

Generates 3 short, punchy hook lines based on your song. Each hook is 15 seconds or less when spoken aloud. Available on all plans.

How to Use
  1. Generate a song.
  2. Click TikTok Hooks in the action bar above the lyrics.
  3. Three hooks appear, each from a different emotional angle.
  4. Click Copy on any hook to copy it to clipboard.
Song Gallery ▼
What is the Gallery?

A public space where users share songs, vote on favorites, and discover new music from around the world.

Publishing Your Song

Go to Song History, find your song, and toggle the Publish button on. Your song appears in the Gallery with your username, genre, mood tags, vote count, and an audio player if you've saved a Suno audio URL.

Voting

Click the flame 🔥 icon on any song to vote. Songs appear in This Week's Top 10 (rolling 7-day window) and Hall of Fame (all-time top songs).

Filtering

Use the genre filter tabs or search bar to find songs by title. Unpublish at any time by toggling Publish off.

Shareable Song URLs ▼
Your Song's Public URL

Every published song gets a permanent public URL at writemylyrics.ai/song/[id]. No login required to view.

How to Share
  1. Publish your song (toggle Publish on in the Song Notes panel).
  2. Go to Song History.
  3. Click the Share button on the song card.
  4. The URL is copied to your clipboard automatically.
What Visitors See

Title, author name, genre, full lyrics, vote count, audio player (if audio URL is saved), and a Write Your Own call to action.

Co-writer Mode ▼
Plan Required

Co-writer Mode is available on Pro and Unlimited plans.

Inviting a Co-writer
  1. Go to Song History.
  2. Click Co-writers on any song card.
  3. Enter your collaborator's email address.
  4. Send the invite. They'll see the shared song in their own Song History.
Suggesting Changes

Co-writers click Suggest Changes on the shared song, write their lyric revision and an optional comment, then submit.

Reviewing Suggestions

The song owner clicks Suggestions on the song card to review each submission. Click Accept to apply the new lyrics or Reject to dismiss.
